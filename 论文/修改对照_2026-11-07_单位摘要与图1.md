## 5 英文摘要

**修改前**

Earth system models rely on gridded data at regional to global scales for parameter initialization and evaluation of simulation results. Although most datasets are available in standard formats such as NetCDF, HDF, and GeoTIFF, data sources still differ in processing rules, units, data identifiers, and distribution methods. Researchers therefore often need to organize and maintain databases for individual models, adding to the work required for continued model development and evaluation. Building on the 2022 release of GriddingMachine, this study improves data preprocessing, distribution, and reading. Shared configurations organize processing rules so that data from different sources can be standardized according to common conventions. Direct NetCDF distribution and an independently maintained catalog simplify data acquisition and updates, while land parameters and meteorological forcing are assembled from multiple sources to provide model inputs. Application cases show that the improved workflow maintains agreement between standardized results and independent references, shortens the time from data acquisition to the first read, preserves access through existing labels after data revisions, and connects data from multiple sources to model inputs. Automatic downloading of the updated data has also been improved for MATLAB, Octave, Python, and R, with new support for C and Fortran, to better serve scientific research and applications.

**修改后**

Earth system models depend on gridded data at regional to global scales for parameter initialization and evaluation of simulation results. Although most datasets use standard formats such as NetCDF, HDF, and GeoTIFF, differences in processing rules, units, data identifiers, and distribution methods still require researchers to organize and maintain databases for individual models, adding to the work of continued model development and evaluation. Building on GriddingMachine v0.2 released in 2022, the updated framework (v0.5) improves data preprocessing, distribution, and reading. A standardized processing workflow enables data providers to prepare and verify their datasets. Regularly updated database catalogs are archived on Zenodo to make each catalog version traceable, while NetCDF data files are shared through Zenodo and ScienceDB to facilitate access. Reading interfaces support collections of data from multiple sources, allowing initialization parameters and meteorological forcing for a land surface model to be obtained in a single call. The updated framework separates data input and output operations, including processing and reading, from the distribution of catalogs and data files, so that new data can be added independently of software releases. Automatic downloading of the updated data has also been improved for Matlab, Octave, Python, and R, with new support for C and Fortran, to better serve Earth science research and applications.

## 6 Google Earth Engine及本地数据库需求

**修改前**

尽管Google Earth Engine等云平台通过改善数据表达、发现、访问及计算，提升了大尺度地学数据的访问和分析能力[4]，但是大多数社区生产的数据仍并接入这些云平台。此外，这些云平台并不提供直接的云端数据和本地数据同步功能，忽视了用户进行本地数据利用的需求。针对该问题，我们需要标准化的数据转换规则和数据标识、自动化的网络数据获取。Wang等[5]于2022年做了初步尝试，发布了基于Julia语言的GriddingMachine数据库和软件（v0.2）。Wang等[5]首先将常用于地球系统模式的的全球网格化数据整理为具有统一空间和变量约定的NetCDF文件，然后为每个数据被分配了一个独一无二且易读懂的数据标签，最后将数据标签与下载地址保存在GriddingMachine.jl软件包内的Artifacts.toml文件中，以便实现数据自动下载。

**修改后**

Google Earth Engine通过改善数据表达、发现、访问及计算，提升了大尺度地学数据的访问和分析能力[4]。在将这些数据用于本地地球系统模式时，研究人员仍需根据模式要求统一网格、变量和单位，并在源数据更新后维护处理规则、数据版本与模式输入之间的对应关系。因此，标准化的数据转换规则、清晰的数据标识和自动化的数据获取，是衔接数据共享与本地模式应用的重要基础。Wang等[5]于2022年做了初步尝试，发布了基于Julia语言的GriddingMachine数据库和软件（v0.2）。Wang等[5]首先将常用于地球系统模式的全球网格化数据整理为具有统一空间和变量约定的NetCDF文件，随后为各数据分配唯一且易于识别的数据标签，最后将数据标签与下载地址保存在GriddingMachine.jl软件包内的Artifacts.toml文件中，以便实现数据自动下载。

## 9 模块协作及远程请求关系

**修改前**

三个功能层通过标准NetCDF和数据标签连接。生产流程输出以标签命名的文件，数据目录记录标签对应的文件地址，科研程序据此获取和读取数据。源数据变化时更新处理配置，分发位置变化时调整目录，模式程序则沿用统一读取方式，并根据自身接口组织输入。这样，处理规则、分发信息和模式接入程序可以分别维护。具体方法见第2.2—2.4节。

**修改后**

各模块通过标准NetCDF文件和数据标签协同工作。Cartographer完成标准化处理，Collector依据独立数据目录获取与缓存文件，Indexer读取数据并组织模式输入。需要按位置远程访问数据时，GMServer在服务端调用Collector与Indexer，Requestor向其发送标签、经纬度和周期信息并接收格点数据。数据处理规则、分发信息和模式接入程序由此可以分别维护。
